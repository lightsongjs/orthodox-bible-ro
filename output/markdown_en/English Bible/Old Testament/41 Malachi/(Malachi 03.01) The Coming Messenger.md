# The Coming Messenger

3. See, I am sending my messenger to prepare the way before me, and the Lord whom you seek will suddenly come to his temple. The messenger of the covenant in whom you delight—indeed, he is coming, says theLordof hosts.
2. But who can endure the day of his coming, and who can stand when he appears?
3. he will sit as a refiner and purifier of silver, and he will purify the descendants of Levi and refine them like gold and silver, until they present offerings to theLordin righteousness.
4. Then the offering of Judah and Jerusalem will be pleasing to theLordas in the days of old and as in former years.
5. Then I will draw near to you for judgment; I will be swift to bear witness against the sorcerers, against the adulterers, against those who swear falsely, against those who oppress the hired workers in their wages, the widow and the orphan, against those who thrust aside the alien, and do not fear me, says theLordof hosts.
6. For I theLorddo not change; therefore you, O children of Jacob, have not perished.
7. Ever since the days of your ancestors you have turned aside from my statutes and have not kept them. Return to me, and I will return to you, says theLordof hosts. But you say, “How shall we return?”
