# Proclamation against Tyre

26. In the eleventh year, on the first day of the month, the word of theLordcame to me:
2. Mortal, because Tyre said concerning Jerusalem,
3. therefore, thus says the LordGod:
4. They shall destroy the walls of Tyre
5. It shall become, in the midst of the sea,
6. and its daughter-towns in the country
7. For thus says the LordGod: I will bring against Tyre from the north King Nebuchadrezzar of Babylon, king of kings, together with horses, chariots, cavalry, and a great and powerful army.
8. Your daughter-towns in the country
9. He shall direct the shock of his battering rams against your walls
10. His horses shall be so many
11. With the hoofs of his horses
12. They will plunder your riches
13. I will silence the music of your songs;
14. I will make you a bare rock;
15. Thus says the LordGodto Tyre: Shall not the coastlands shake at the sound of your fall, when the wounded groan, when slaughter goes on within you?
16. Then all the princes of the sea shall step down from their thrones; they shall remove their robes and strip off their embroidered garments. They shall clothe themselves with trembling, and shall sit on the ground; they shall tremble every moment, and be appalled at you.
17. And they shall raise a lamentation over you, and say to you:
18. Now the coastlands tremble
19. For thus says the LordGod: When I make you a city laid waste, like cities that are not inhabited, when I bring up the deep over you, and the great waters cover you,
20. then I will thrust you down with those who descend into the Pit, to the people of long ago, and I will make you live in the world below, among primeval ruins, with those who go down to the Pit, so that you will not be inhabited or have a placein the land of the living.
21. I will bring you to a dreadful end, and you shall be no more; though sought for, you will never be found again, says the LordGod.
