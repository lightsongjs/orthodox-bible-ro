# The Boiling Pot

24. In the ninth year, in the tenth month, on the tenth day of the month, the word of theLordcame to me:
2. Mortal, write down the name of this day, this very day. The king of Babylon has laid siege to Jerusalem this very day.
3. And utter an allegory to the rebellious house and say to them, Thus says the LordGod:
4. put in it the pieces,
5. Take the choicest one of the flock,
6. Therefore thus says the LordGod:
7. For the blood she shed is inside it;
8. To rouse my wrath, to take vengeance,
9. Therefore thus says the LordGod:
10. Heap up the logs, kindle the fire;
11. Stand it empty upon the coals,
12. In vain I have wearied myself;
13. Yet, when I cleansed you in your filthy lewdness,
14. I theLordhave spoken; the time is coming, I will act. I will not refrain, I will not spare, I will not relent. According to your ways and your doings I will judge you, says the LordGod.
