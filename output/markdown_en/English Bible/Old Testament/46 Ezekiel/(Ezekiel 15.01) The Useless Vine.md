# The Useless Vine

15. The word of theLordcame to me:
2. O mortal, how does the wood of the vine surpass all other wood—
3. Is wood taken from it to make anything?
4. It is put in the fire for fuel;
5. When it was whole it was used for nothing;
6. Therefore thus says the LordGod: Like the wood of the vine among the trees of the forest, which I have given to the fire for fuel, so I will give up the inhabitants of Jerusalem.
7. I will set my face against them; although they escape from the fire, the fire shall still consume them; and you shall know that I am theLord, when I set my face against them.
8. And I will make the land desolate, because they have acted faithlessly, says the LordGod.
