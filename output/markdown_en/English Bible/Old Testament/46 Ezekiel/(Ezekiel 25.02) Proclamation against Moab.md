# Proclamation against Moab

8. Thus says the LordGod: Because Moabsaid, The house of Judah is like all the other nations,
9. therefore I will lay open the flank of Moab from the townson its frontier, the glory of the country, Beth-jeshimoth, Baal-meon, and Kiriathaim.
10. I will give it along with Ammon to the people of the east as a possession. Thus Ammon shall be remembered no more among the nations,
11. and I will execute judgments upon Moab. Then they shall know that I am theLord.
