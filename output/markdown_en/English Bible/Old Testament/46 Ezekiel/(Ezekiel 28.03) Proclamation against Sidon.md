# Proclamation against Sidon

20. The word of theLordcame to me:
21. Mortal, set your face toward Sidon, and prophesy against it,
22. and say, Thus says the LordGod:
23. for I will send pestilence into it,
24. The house of Israel shall no longer find a pricking brier or a piercing thorn among all their neighbors who have treated them with contempt. And they shall know that I am the LordGod.
