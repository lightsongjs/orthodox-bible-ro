# Lamentation over the King of Tyre

11. Moreover the word of theLordcame to me:
12. Mortal, raise a lamentation over the king of Tyre, and say to him, Thus says the LordGod:
13. You were in Eden, the garden of God;
14. With an anointed cherub as guardian I placed you;
15. You were blameless in your ways
16. In the abundance of your trade
17. Your heart was proud because of your beauty;
18. By the multitude of your iniquities,
19. All who know you among the peoples
