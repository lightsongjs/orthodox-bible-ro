# Hezekiah’s Prayer

14. Hezekiah received the letter from the hand of the messengers and read it; then Hezekiah went up to the house of theLordand spread it before theLord.
15. And Hezekiah prayed before theLord, and said: “OLordthe God of Israel, who are enthroned above the cherubim, you are God, you alone, of all the kingdoms of the earth; you have made heaven and earth.
16. Incline your ear, OLord, and hear; open your eyes, OLord, and see; hear the words of Sennacherib, which he has sent to mock the living God.
17. Truly, OLord, the kings of Assyria have laid waste the nations and their lands,
18. and have hurled their gods into the fire, though they were no gods but the work of human hands—wood and stone—and so they were destroyed.
19. So now, OLordour God, save us, I pray you, from his hand, so that all the kingdoms of the earth may know that you, OLord, are God alone.”
20. Then Isaiah son of Amoz sent to Hezekiah, saying, “Thus says theLord, the God of Israel: I have heard your prayer to me about King Sennacherib of Assyria.
21. This is the word that theLordhas spoken concerning him:
22. “Whom have you mocked and reviled?
23. By your messengers you have mocked the Lord,
24. I dug wells
25. “Have you not heard
26. while their inhabitants, shorn of strength,
27. “But I know your risingand your sitting,
28. Because you have raged against me
29. “And this shall be the sign for you: This year you shall eat what grows of itself, and in the second year what springs from that; then in the third year sow, reap, plant vineyards, and eat their fruit.
30. The surviving remnant of the house of Judah shall again take root downward, and bear fruit upward;
31. for from Jerusalem a remnant shall go out, and from Mount Zion a band of survivors. The zeal of theLordof hosts will do this.
32. “Therefore thus says theLordconcerning the king of Assyria: He shall not come into this city, shoot an arrow there, come before it with a shield, or cast up a siege ramp against it.
33. By the way that he came, by the same he shall return; he shall not come into this city, says theLord.
34. For I will defend this city to save it, for my own sake and for the sake of my servant David.”
