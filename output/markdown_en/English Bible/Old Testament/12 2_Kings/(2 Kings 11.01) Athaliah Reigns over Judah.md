# Athaliah Reigns over Judah

11. Now when Athaliah, Ahaziah’s mother, saw that her son was dead, she set about to destroy all the royal family.
2. But Jehosheba, King Joram’s daughter, Ahaziah’s sister, took Joash son of Ahaziah, and stole him away from among the king’s children who were about to be killed; she puthim and his nurse in a bedroom. Thus shehid him from Athaliah, so that he was not killed;
3. he remained with her six years, hidden in the house of theLord, while Athaliah reigned over the land.
