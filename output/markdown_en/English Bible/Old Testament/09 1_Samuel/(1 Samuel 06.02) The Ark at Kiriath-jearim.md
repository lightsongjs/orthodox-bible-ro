# The Ark at Kiriath-jearim

19. The descendants of Jeconiah did not rejoice with the people of Beth-shemesh when they greetedthe ark of theLord; and he killed seventy men of them.The people mourned because theLordhad made a great slaughter among the people.
20. Then the people of Beth-shemesh said, “Who is able to stand before theLord, this holy God? To whom shall he go so that we may be rid of him?”
21. So they sent messengers to the inhabitants of Kiriath-jearim, saying, “The Philistines have returned the ark of theLord. Come down and take it up to you.”
