# Locusts, Fire, and a Plumb Line

7. This is what the LordGodshowed me: he was forming locusts at the time the latter growth began to sprout (it was the latter growth after the king’s mowings).
2. When they had finished eating the grass of the land, I said,
3. TheLordrelented concerning this;
4. This is what the LordGodshowed me: the LordGodwas calling for a shower of fire,and it devoured the great deep and was eating up the land.
5. Then I said,
6. TheLordrelented concerning this;
7. This is what he showed me: the Lord was standing beside a wall built with a plumb line, with a plumb line in his hand.
8. And theLordsaid to me, “Amos, what do you see?” And I said, “A plumb line.” Then the Lord said,
