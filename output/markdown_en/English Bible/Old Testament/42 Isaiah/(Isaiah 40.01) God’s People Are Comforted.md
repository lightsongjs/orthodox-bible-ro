# God’s People Are Comforted

40. Comfort, O comfort my people,
2. Speak tenderly to Jerusalem,
3. A voice cries out:
4. Every valley shall be lifted up,
5. Then the glory of theLordshall be revealed,
6. A voice says, “Cry out!”
7. The grass withers, the flower fades,
8. The grass withers, the flower fades;
9. Get you up to a high mountain,
10. See, the LordGodcomes with might,
11. He will feed his flock like a shepherd;
12. Who has measured the waters in the hollow of his hand
13. Who has directed the spirit of theLord,
14. Whom did he consult for his enlightenment,
15. Even the nations are like a drop from a bucket,
16. Lebanon would not provide fuel enough,
17. All the nations are as nothing before him;
18. To whom then will you liken God,
19. An idol? —A workman casts it,
20. As a gift one chooses mulberry wood
21. Have you not known? Have you not heard?
22. It is he who sits above the circle of the earth,
23. who brings princes to naught,
24. Scarcely are they planted, scarcely sown,
25. To whom then will you compare me,
26. Lift up your eyes on high and see:
27. Why do you say, O Jacob,
28. Have you not known? Have you not heard?
29. He gives power to the faint,
30. Even youths will faint and be weary,
31. but those who wait for theLordshall renew their strength,
