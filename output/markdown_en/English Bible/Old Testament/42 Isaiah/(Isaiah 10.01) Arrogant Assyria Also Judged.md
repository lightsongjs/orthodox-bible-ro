# Arrogant Assyria Also Judged

5. Ah, Assyria, the rod of my anger—
6. Against a godless nation I send him,
7. But this is not what he intends,
8. For he says:
9. Is not Calno like Carchemish?
10. As my hand has reached to the kingdoms of the idols
11. shall I not do to Jerusalem and her idols
12. When the Lord has finished all his work on Mount Zion and on Jerusalem, hewill punish the arrogant boasting of the king of Assyria and his haughty pride.
13. For he says:
14. My hand has found, like a nest,
15. Shall the ax vaunt itself over the one who wields it,
16. Therefore the Sovereign, theLordof hosts,
17. The light of Israel will become a fire,
18. The glory of his forest and his fruitful land
19. The remnant of the trees of his forest will be so few
