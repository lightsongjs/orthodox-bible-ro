# A Hymn of Praise

10. Sing to theLorda new song,
11. Let the desert and its towns lift up their voice,
12. Let them give glory to theLord,
13. TheLordgoes forth like a soldier,
14. For a long time I have held my peace,
15. I will lay waste mountains and hills,
16. I will lead the blind
17. They shall be turned back and utterly put to shame—
18. Listen, you that are deaf;
19. Who is blind but my servant,
20. He sees many things, but doesnot observe them;
