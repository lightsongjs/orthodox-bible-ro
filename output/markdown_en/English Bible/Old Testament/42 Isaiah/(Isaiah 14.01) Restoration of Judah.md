# Restoration of Judah

14. But theLordwill have compassion on Jacob and will again choose Israel, and will set them in their own land; and aliens will join them and attach themselves to the house of Jacob.
2. And the nations will take them and bring them to their place, and the house of Israel will possess the nationsas male and female slaves in theLord’s land; they will take captive those who were their captors, and rule over those who oppressed them.
