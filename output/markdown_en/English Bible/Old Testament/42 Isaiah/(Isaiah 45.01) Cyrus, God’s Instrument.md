# Cyrus, God’s Instrument

45. Thus says theLordto his anointed, to Cyrus,
2. I will go before you
3. I will give you the treasures of darkness
4. For the sake of my servant Jacob,
5. I am theLord, and there is no other;
6. so that they may know, from the rising of the sun
7. I form light and create darkness,
8. Shower, O heavens, from above,
9. Woe to you who strive with your Maker,
10. Woe to anyone who says to a father, “What are you begetting?”
11. Thus says theLord,
12. I made the earth,
13. I have aroused Cyrusin righteousness,
14. Thus says theLord:
15. Truly, you are a God who hides himself,
16. All of them are put to shame and confounded,
17. But Israel is saved by theLord
18. For thus says theLord,
19. I did not speak in secret,
