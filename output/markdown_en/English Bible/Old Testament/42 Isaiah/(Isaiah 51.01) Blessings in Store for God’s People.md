# Blessings in Store for God’s People

51. Listen to me, you that pursue righteousness,
2. Look to Abraham your father
3. For theLordwill comfort Zion;
4. Listen to me, my people,
5. I will bring near my deliverance swiftly,
6. Lift up your eyes to the heavens,
7. Listen to me, you who know righteousness,
8. For the moth will eat them up like a garment,
9. Awake, awake, put on strength,
10. Was it not you who dried up the sea,
11. So the ransomed of theLordshall return,
12. I, I am he who comforts you;
13. You have forgotten theLord, your Maker,
14. The oppressed shall speedily be released;
15. For I am theLordyour God,
16. I have put my words in your mouth,
17. Rouse yourself, rouse yourself!
18. There is no one to guide her
19. These two things have befallen you
20. Your children have fainted,
21. Therefore hear this, you who are wounded,
22. Thus says your Sovereign, theLord,
23. And I will put it into the hand of your tormentors,
