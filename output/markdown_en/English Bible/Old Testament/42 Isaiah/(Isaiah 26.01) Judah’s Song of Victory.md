# Judah’s Song of Victory

26. On that day this song will be sung in the land of Judah:
2. Open the gates,
3. Those of steadfast mind you keep in peace—
4. Trust in theLordforever,
5. For he has brought low
6. The foot tramples it,
7. The way of the righteous is level;
8. In the path of your judgments,
9. My soul yearns for you in the night,
10. If favor is shown to the wicked,
11. OLord, your hand is lifted up,
12. OLord, you will ordain peace for us,
13. OLordour God,
14. The dead do not live;
15. But you have increased the nation, OLord,
16. OLord, in distress they sought you,
17. Like a woman with child,
18. we were with child, we writhed,
19. Your dead shall live, their corpsesshall rise.
20. Come, my people, enter your chambers,
21. For theLordcomes out from his place
