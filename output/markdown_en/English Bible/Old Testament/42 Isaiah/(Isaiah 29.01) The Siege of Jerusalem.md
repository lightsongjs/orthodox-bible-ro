# The Siege of Jerusalem

29. Ah, Ariel, Ariel,
2. Yet I will distress Ariel,
3. And like DavidI will encamp against you;
4. Then deep from the earth you shall speak,
5. But the multitude of your foesshall be like small dust,
6. you will be visited by theLordof hosts
7. And the multitude of all the nations that fight against Ariel,
8. Just as when a hungry person dreams of eating
9. Stupefy yourselves and be in a stupor,
10. For theLordhas poured out upon you
11. The vision of all this has become for you like the words of a sealed document. If it is given to those who can read, with the command, “Read this,” they say, “We cannot, for it is sealed.”
12. And if it is given to those who cannot read, saying, “Read this,” they say, “We cannot read.”
13. The Lord said:
14. so I will again do
15. Ha! You who hide a plan too deep for theLord,
16. You turn things upside down!
