# Judgment on the Nations

34. Draw near, O nations, to hear;
2. For theLordis enraged against all the nations,
3. Their slain shall be cast out,
4. All the host of heaven shall rot away,
5. When my sword has drunk its fill in the heavens,
6. TheLordhas a sword; it is sated with blood,
7. Wild oxen shall fall with them,
8. For theLordhas a day of vengeance,
9. And the streams of Edomshall be turned into pitch,
10. Night and day it shall not be quenched;
11. But the hawkand the hedgehogshall possess it;
12. They shall name it No Kingdom There,
13. Thorns shall grow over its strongholds,
14. Wildcats shall meet with hyenas,
15. There shall the owl nest
16. Seek and read from the book of theLord:
17. He has cast the lot for them,
