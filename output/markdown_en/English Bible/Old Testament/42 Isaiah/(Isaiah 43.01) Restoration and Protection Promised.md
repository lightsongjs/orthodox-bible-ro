# Restoration and Protection Promised

43. But now thus says theLord,
2. When you pass through the waters, I will be with you;
3. For I am theLordyour God,
4. Because you are precious in my sight,
5. Do not fear, for I am with you;
6. I will say to the north, “Give them up,”
7. everyone who is called by my name,
8. Bring forth the people who are blind, yet have eyes,
9. Let all the nations gather together,
10. You are my witnesses, says theLord,
11. I, I am theLord,
12. I declared and saved and proclaimed,
13. I am God, and also henceforth I am He;
14. Thus says theLord,
15. I am theLord, your Holy One,
16. Thus says theLord,
17. who brings out chariot and horse,
18. Do not remember the former things,
19. I am about to do a new thing;
20. The wild animals will honor me,
21. the people whom I formed for myself
22. Yet you did not call upon me, O Jacob;
23. You have not brought me your sheep for burnt offerings,
24. You have not bought me sweet cane with money,
25. I, I am He
26. Accuse me, let us go to trial;
27. Your first ancestor sinned,
