# The Journey to Moab

10. The Israelites set out, and camped in Oboth.
11. They set out from Oboth, and camped at Iye-abarim, in the wilderness bordering Moab toward the sunrise.
12. From there they set out, and camped in the Wadi Zered.
13. From there they set out, and camped on the other side of the Arnon, inthe wilderness that extends from the boundary of the Amorites; for the Arnon is the boundary of Moab, between Moab and the Amorites.
