# The Sons of Aaron

3. This is the lineage of Aaron and Moses at the time when theLordspoke with Moses on Mount Sinai.
2. These are the names of the sons of Aaron: Nadab the firstborn, and Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar;
3. these are the names of the sons of Aaron, the anointed priests, whom he ordained to minister as priests.
4. Nadab and Abihu died before theLordwhen they offered unholy fire before theLordin the wilderness of Sinai, and they had no children. Eleazar and Ithamar served as priests in the lifetime of their father Aaron.
