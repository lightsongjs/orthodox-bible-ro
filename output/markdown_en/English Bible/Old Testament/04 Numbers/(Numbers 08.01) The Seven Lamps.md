# The Seven Lamps

8. TheLordspoke to Moses, saying:
2. Speak to Aaron and say to him: When you set up the lamps, the seven lamps shall give light in front of the lampstand.
3. Aaron did so; he set up its lamps to give light in front of the lampstand, as theLordhad commanded Moses.
4. Now this was how the lampstand was made, out of hammered work of gold. From its base to its flowers, it was hammered work; according to the pattern that theLordhad shown Moses, so he made the lampstand.
