# The People Rebel

14. Then all the congregation raised a loud cry, and the people wept that night.
2. And all the Israelites complained against Moses and Aaron; the whole congregation said to them, “Would that we had died in the land of Egypt! Or would that we had died in this wilderness!
3. Why is theLordbringing us into this land to fall by the sword? Our wives and our little ones will become booty; would it not be better for us to go back to Egypt?”
4. So they said to one another, “Let us choose a captain, and go back to Egypt.”
5. Then Moses and Aaron fell on their faces before all the assembly of the congregation of the Israelites.
6. And Joshua son of Nun and Caleb son of Jephunneh, who were among those who had spied out the land, tore their clothes
7. and said to all the congregation of the Israelites, “The land that we went through as spies is an exceedingly good land.
8. If theLordis pleased with us, he will bring us into this land and give it to us, a land that flows with milk and honey.
9. Only, do not rebel against theLord; and do not fear the people of the land, for they are no more than bread for us; their protection is removed from them, and theLordis with us; do not fear them.”
10. But the whole congregation threatened to stone them.
11. And theLordsaid to Moses, “How long will this people despise me? And how long will they refuse to believe in me, in spite of all the signs that I have done among them?
12. I will strike them with pestilence and disinherit them, and I will make of you a nation greater and mightier than they.”
