# Ahaziah Reigns over Israel

51. Ahaziah son of Ahab began to reign over Israel in Samaria in the seventeenth year of King Jehoshaphat of Judah; he reigned two years over Israel.
52. He did what was evil in the sight of theLord, and walked in the way of his father and mother, and in the way of Jeroboam son of Nebat, who caused Israel to sin.
53. He served Baal and worshiped him; he provoked theLord, the God of Israel, to anger, just as his father had done.
