# Death of Solomon

41. Now the rest of the acts of Solomon, all that he did as well as his wisdom, are they not written in the Book of the Acts of Solomon?
42. The time that Solomon reigned in Jerusalem over all Israel was forty years.
43. Solomon slept with his ancestors and was buried in the city of his father David; and his son Rehoboam succeeded him.
