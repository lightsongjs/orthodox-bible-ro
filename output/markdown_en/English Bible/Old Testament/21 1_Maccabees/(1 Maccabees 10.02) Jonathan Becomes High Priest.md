# Jonathan Becomes High Priest

18. “King Alexander to his brother Jonathan, greetings.
19. We have heard about you, that you are a mighty warrior and worthy to be our friend.
20. And so we have appointed you today to be the high priest of your nation; you are to be called the king’s Friend and you are to take our side and keep friendship with us.” He also sent him a purple robe and a golden crown.
21. So Jonathan put on the sacred vestments in the seventh month of the one hundred sixtieth year,at the festival of booths,and he recruited troops and equipped them with arms in abundance.
22. When Demetrius heard of these things he was distressed and said,
23. “What is this that we have done? Alexander has gotten ahead of us in forming a friendship with the Jews to strengthen himself.
24. I also will write them words of encouragement and promise them honor and gifts, so that I may have their help.”
