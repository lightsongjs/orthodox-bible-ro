# Moses and Aaron Obey God’s Commands

28. On the day when theLordspoke to Moses in the land of Egypt,
29. he said to him, “I am theLord; tell Pharaoh king of Egypt all that I am speaking to you.”
30. But Moses said in theLord’s presence, “Since I am a poor speaker,why would Pharaoh listen to me?”
