# The Song of Moses

15. Then Moses and the Israelites sang this song to theLord:
2. TheLordis my strength and my might,
3. TheLordis a warrior;
4. “Pharaoh’s chariots and his army he cast into the sea;
5. The floods covered them;
6. Your right hand, OLord, glorious in power—
7. In the greatness of your majesty you overthrew your adversaries;
8. At the blast of your nostrils the waters piled up,
9. The enemy said, ‘I will pursue, I will overtake,
10. You blew with your wind, the sea covered them;
11. “Who is like you, OLord, among the gods?
12. You stretched out your right hand,
13. “In your steadfast love you led the people whom you redeemed;
14. The peoples heard, they trembled;
15. Then the chiefs of Edom were dismayed;
16. Terror and dread fell upon them;
17. You brought them in and planted them on the mountain of your own possession,
18. TheLordwill reign forever and ever.”
19. When the horses of Pharaoh with his chariots and his chariot drivers went into the sea, theLordbrought back the waters of the sea upon them; but the Israelites walked through the sea on dry ground.
