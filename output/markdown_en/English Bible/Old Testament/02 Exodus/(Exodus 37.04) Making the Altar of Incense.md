# Making the Altar of Incense

25. He made the altar of incense of acacia wood, one cubit long, and one cubit wide; it was square, and was two cubits high; its horns were of one piece with it.
26. He overlaid it with pure gold, its top, and its sides all around, and its horns; and he made for it a molding of gold all around,
27. and made two golden rings for it under its molding, on two opposite sides of it, to hold the poles with which to carry it.
28. And he made the poles of acacia wood, and overlaid them with gold.
