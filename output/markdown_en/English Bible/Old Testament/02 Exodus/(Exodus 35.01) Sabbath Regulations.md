# Sabbath Regulations

35. Moses assembled all the congregation of the Israelites and said to them: These are the things that theLordhas commanded you to do:
2. Six days shall work be done, but on the seventh day you shall have a holy sabbath of solemn rest to theLord; whoever does any work on it shall be put to death.
3. You shall kindle no fire in all your dwellings on the sabbath day.
