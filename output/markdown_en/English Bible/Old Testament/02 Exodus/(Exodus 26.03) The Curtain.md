# The Curtain

31. You shall make a curtain of blue, purple, and crimson yarns, and of fine twisted linen; it shall be made with cherubim skillfully worked into it.
32. You shall hang it on four pillars of acacia overlaid with gold, which have hooks of gold and rest on four bases of silver.
33. You shall hang the curtain under the clasps, and bring the ark of the covenantin there, within the curtain; and the curtain shall separate for you the holy place from the most holy.
34. You shall put the mercy seaton the ark of the covenantin the most holy place.
35. You shall set the table outside the curtain, and the lampstand on the south side of the tabernacle opposite the table; and you shall put the table on the north side.
36. You shall make a screen for the entrance of the tent, of blue, purple, and crimson yarns, and of fine twisted linen, embroidered with needlework.
37. You shall make for the screen five pillars of acacia, and overlay them with gold; their hooks shall be of gold, and you shall cast five bases of bronze for them.
