# Israel’s Infidelity, Punishment, and Redemption

2. Plead with your mother, plead—
3. or I will strip her naked
4. Upon her children also I will have no pity,
5. For their mother has played the whore;
6. Therefore I will hedge up herway with thorns;
7. She shall pursue her lovers,
8. She did not know
9. Therefore I will take back
10. Now I will uncover her shame
11. I will put an end to all her mirth,
12. I will lay waste her vines and her fig trees,
13. I will punish her for the festival days of the Baals,
14. Therefore, I will now allure her,
15. From there I will give her her vineyards,
16. On that day, says theLord, you will call me, “My husband,” and no longer will you call me, “My Baal.”
17. For I will remove the names of the Baals from her mouth, and they shall be mentioned by name no more.
18. I will make for youa covenant on that day with the wild animals, the birds of the air, and the creeping things of the ground; and I will abolishthe bow, the sword, and war from the land; and I will make you lie down in safety.
19. And I will take you for my wife forever; I will take you for my wife in righteousness and in justice, in steadfast love, and in mercy.
20. I will take you for my wife in faithfulness; and you shall know theLord.
21. On that day I will answer, says theLord,
22. and the earth shall answer the grain, the wine, and the oil,
23. and I will sow himfor myself in the land.
