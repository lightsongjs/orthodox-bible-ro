# A Call to Repentance and Prayer

13. Put on sackcloth and lament, you priests;
14. Sanctify a fast,
15. Alas for the day!
16. Is not the food cut off
17. The seed shrivels under the clods,
18. How the animals groan!
19. To you, OLord, I cry.
