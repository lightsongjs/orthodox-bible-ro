# God’s Spirit Poured Out

28. Then afterward
29. Even on the male and female slaves,
30. I will show portents in the heavens and on the earth, blood and fire and columns of smoke.
31. The sun shall be turned to darkness, and the moon to blood, before the great and terrible day of theLordcomes.
32. Then everyone who calls on the name of theLordshall be saved; for in Mount Zion and in Jerusalem there shall be those who escape, as theLordhas said, and among the survivors shall be those whom theLordcalls.
