# Admonition to Trust and Honor God

3. My child, do not forget my teaching,
2. for length of days and years of life
3. Do not let loyalty and faithfulness forsake you;
4. So you will find favor and good repute
5. Trust in theLordwith all your heart,
6. In all your ways acknowledge him,
7. Do not be wise in your own eyes;
8. It will be a healing for your flesh
9. Honor theLordwith your substance
10. then your barns will be filled with plenty,
11. My child, do not despise theLord’s discipline
12. for theLordreproves the one he loves,
