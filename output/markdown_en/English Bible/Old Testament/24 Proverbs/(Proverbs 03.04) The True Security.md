# The True Security

21. My child, do not let these escape from your sight:
22. and they will be life for your soul
23. Then you will walk on your way securely
24. If you sit down,you will not be afraid;
25. Do not be afraid of sudden panic,
26. for theLordwill be your confidence
27. Do not withhold good from those to whom it is due,
28. Do not say to your neighbor, “Go, and come again,
29. Do not plan harm against your neighbor
30. Do not quarrel with anyone without cause,
31. Do not envy the violent
32. for the perverse are an abomination to theLord,
33. TheLord’s curse is on the house of the wicked,
34. Toward the scorners he is scornful,
35. The wise will inherit honor,
