# A Plea for Mercy

5. Remember, OLord, what has befallen us;
2. Our inheritance has been turned over to strangers,
3. We have become orphans, fatherless;
4. We must pay for the water we drink;
5. With a yokeon our necks we are hard driven;
6. We have made a pact withEgypt and Assyria,
7. Our ancestors sinned; they are no more,
8. Slaves rule over us;
9. We get our bread at the peril of our lives,
10. Our skin is black as an oven
11. Women are raped in Zion,
12. Princes are hung up by their hands;
13. Young men are compelled to grind,
14. The old men have left the city gate,
15. The joy of our hearts has ceased;
16. The crown has fallen from our head;
17. Because of this our hearts are sick,
18. because of Mount Zion, which lies desolate;
19. But you, OLord, reign forever;
20. Why have you forgotten us completely?
21. Restore us to yourself, OLord, that we may be restored;
22. unless you have utterly rejected us,
