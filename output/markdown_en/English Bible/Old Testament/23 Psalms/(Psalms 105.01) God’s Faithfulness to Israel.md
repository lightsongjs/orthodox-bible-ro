# God’s Faithfulness to Israel

1. O give thanks to theLord, call on his name,
2. Sing to him, sing praises to him;
3. Glory in his holy name;
4. Seek theLordand his strength;
5. Remember the wonderful works he has done,
6. O offspring of his servant Abraham,
7. He is theLordour God;
8. He is mindful of his covenant forever,
9. the covenant that he made with Abraham,
10. which he confirmed to Jacob as a statute,
11. saying, “To you I will give the land of Canaan
12. When they were few in number,
13. wandering from nation to nation,
14. he allowed no one to oppress them;
15. saying, “Do not touch my anointed ones;
16. When he summoned famine against the land,
17. he had sent a man ahead of them,
18. His feet were hurt with fetters,
19. until what he had said came to pass,
20. The king sent and released him;
21. He made him lord of his house,
22. to instructhis officials at his pleasure,
23. Then Israel came to Egypt;
24. And theLordmade his people very fruitful,
25. whose hearts he then turned to hate his people,
26. He sent his servant Moses,
27. They performed his signs among them,
28. He sent darkness, and made the land dark;
29. He turned their waters into blood,
30. Their land swarmed with frogs,
31. He spoke, and there came swarms of flies,
32. He gave them hail for rain,
33. He struck their vines and fig trees,
34. He spoke, and the locusts came,
35. they devoured all the vegetation in their land,
36. He struck down all the firstborn in their land,
37. Then he brought Israelout with silver and gold,
38. Egypt was glad when they departed,
39. He spread a cloud for a covering,
40. They asked, and he brought quails,
41. He opened the rock, and water gushed out;
42. For he remembered his holy promise,
43. So he brought his people out with joy,
44. He gave them the lands of the nations,
45. that they might keep his statutes
