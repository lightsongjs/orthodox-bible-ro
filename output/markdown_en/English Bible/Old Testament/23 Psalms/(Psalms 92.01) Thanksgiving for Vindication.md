# Thanksgiving for Vindication

1. It is good to give thanks to theLord,
2. to declare your steadfast love in the morning,
3. to the music of the lute and the harp,
4. For you, OLord, have made me glad by your work;
5. How great are your works, OLord!
6. The dullard cannot know,
7. though the wicked sprout like grass
8. but you, OLord, are on high forever.
9. For your enemies, OLord,
10. But you have exalted my horn like that of the wild ox;
11. My eyes have seen the downfall of my enemies;
12. The righteous flourish like the palm tree,
13. They are planted in the house of theLord;
14. In old age they still produce fruit;
15. showing that theLordis upright;
