# The Glory of God’s Reign

1. TheLordis king! Let the earth rejoice;
2. Clouds and thick darkness are all around him;
3. Fire goes before him,
4. His lightnings light up the world;
5. The mountains melt like wax before theLord,
6. The heavens proclaim his righteousness;
7. All worshipers of images are put to shame,
8. Zion hears and is glad,
9. For you, OLord, are most high over all the earth;
10. TheLordloves those who hateevil;
11. Light dawnsfor the righteous,
12. Rejoice in theLord, O you righteous,
