# God’s Power and Justice

1. I will give thanks to theLordwith my whole heart;
2. I will be glad and exult in you;
3. When my enemies turned back,
4. For you have maintained my just cause;
5. You have rebuked the nations, you have destroyed the wicked;
6. The enemies have vanished in everlasting ruins;
7. But theLordsits enthroned forever,
8. He judges the world with righteousness;
9. TheLordis a stronghold for the oppressed,
10. And those who know your name put their trust in you,
11. Sing praises to theLord, who dwells in Zion.
12. For he who avenges blood is mindful of them;
13. Be gracious to me, OLord.
14. so that I may recount all your praises,
15. The nations have sunk in the pit that they made;
16. TheLordhas made himself known, he has executed judgment;
17. The wicked shall depart to Sheol,
18. For the needy shall not always be forgotten,
19. Rise up, OLord! Do not let mortals prevail;
20. Put them in fear, OLord;
