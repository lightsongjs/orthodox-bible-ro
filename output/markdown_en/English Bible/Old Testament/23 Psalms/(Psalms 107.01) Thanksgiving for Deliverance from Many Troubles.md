# Thanksgiving for Deliverance from Many Troubles

1. O give thanks to theLord, for he is good;
2. Let the redeemed of theLordsay so,
3. and gathered in from the lands,
4. Some wandered in desert wastes,
5. hungry and thirsty,
6. Then they cried to theLordin their trouble,
7. he led them by a straight way,
8. Let them thank theLordfor his steadfast love,
9. For he satisfies the thirsty,
10. Some sat in darkness and in gloom,
11. for they had rebelled against the words of God,
12. Their hearts were bowed down with hard labor;
13. Then they cried to theLordin their trouble,
14. he brought them out of darkness and gloom,
15. Let them thank theLordfor his steadfast love,
16. For he shatters the doors of bronze,
17. Some were sickthrough their sinful ways,
18. they loathed any kind of food,
19. Then they cried to theLordin their trouble,
20. he sent out his word and healed them,
21. Let them thank theLordfor his steadfast love,
22. And let them offer thanksgiving sacrifices,
23. Some went down to the sea in ships,
24. they saw the deeds of theLord,
25. For he commanded and raised the stormy wind,
26. They mounted up to heaven, they went down to the depths;
27. they reeled and staggered like drunkards,
28. Then they cried to theLordin their trouble,
29. he made the storm be still,
30. Then they were glad because they had quiet,
31. Let them thank theLordfor his steadfast love,
32. Let them extol him in the congregation of the people,
33. He turns rivers into a desert,
34. a fruitful land into a salty waste,
35. He turns a desert into pools of water,
36. And there he lets the hungry live,
37. they sow fields, and plant vineyards,
38. By his blessing they multiply greatly,
39. When they are diminished and brought low
40. he pours contempt on princes
41. but he raises up the needy out of distress,
42. The upright see it and are glad;
43. Let those who are wise give heed to these things,
