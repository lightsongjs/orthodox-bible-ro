# Thanksgiving for Recovery from Illness

1. I love theLord, because he has heard
2. Because he inclined his ear to me,
3. The snares of death encompassed me;
4. Then I called on the name of theLord:
5. Gracious is theLord, and righteous;
6. TheLordprotects the simple;
7. Return, O my soul, to your rest,
8. For you have delivered my soul from death,
9. I walk before theLord
10. I kept my faith, even when I said,
11. I said in my consternation,
12. What shall I return to theLord
13. I will lift up the cup of salvation
14. I will pay my vows to theLord
15. Precious in the sight of theLord
16. OLord, I am your servant;
17. I will offer to you a thanksgiving sacrifice
18. I will pay my vows to theLord
19. in the courts of the house of theLord,
