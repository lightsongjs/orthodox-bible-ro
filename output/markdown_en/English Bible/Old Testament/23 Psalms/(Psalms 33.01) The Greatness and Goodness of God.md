# The Greatness and Goodness of God

1. Rejoice in theLord, O you righteous.
2. Praise theLordwith the lyre;
3. Sing to him a new song;
4. For the word of theLordis upright,
5. He loves righteousness and justice;
6. By the word of theLordthe heavens were made,
7. He gathered the waters of the sea as in a bottle;
8. Let all the earth fear theLord;
9. For he spoke, and it came to be;
10. TheLordbrings the counsel of the nations to nothing;
11. The counsel of theLordstands forever,
12. Happy is the nation whose God is theLord,
13. TheLordlooks down from heaven;
14. From where he sits enthroned he watches
15. he who fashions the hearts of them all,
16. A king is not saved by his great army;
17. The war horse is a vain hope for victory,
18. Truly the eye of theLordis on those who fear him,
19. to deliver their soul from death,
20. Our soul waits for theLord;
21. Our heart is glad in him,
22. Let your steadfast love, OLord, be upon us,
