# Exhortation to Patience and Trust

1. Do not fret because of the wicked;
2. for they will soon fade like the grass,
3. Trust in theLord, and do good;
4. Take delight in theLord,
5. Commit your way to theLord;
6. He will make your vindication shine like the light,
7. Be still before theLord, and wait patiently for him;
8. Refrain from anger, and forsake wrath.
9. For the wicked shall be cut off,
10. Yet a little while, and the wicked will be no more;
11. But the meek shall inherit the land,
12. The wicked plot against the righteous,
13. but theLordlaughs at the wicked,
14. The wicked draw the sword and bend their bows
15. their sword shall enter their own heart,
16. Better is a little that the righteous person has
17. For the arms of the wicked shall be broken,
18. TheLordknows the days of the blameless,
19. they are not put to shame in evil times,
20. But the wicked perish,
21. The wicked borrow, and do not pay back,
22. for those blessed by theLordshall inherit the land,
23. Our stepsare made firm by theLord,
24. though we stumble,weshall not fall headlong,
25. I have been young, and now am old,
26. They are ever giving liberally and lending,
27. Depart from evil, and do good;
28. For theLordloves justice;
29. The righteous shall inherit the land,
30. The mouths of the righteous utter wisdom,
31. The law of their God is in their hearts;
32. The wicked watch for the righteous,
33. TheLordwill not abandon them to their power,
34. Wait for theLord, and keep to his way,
35. I have seen the wicked oppressing,
36. Again Ipassed by, and they were no more;
37. Mark the blameless, and behold the upright,
38. But transgressors shall be altogether destroyed;
39. The salvation of the righteous is from theLord;
40. TheLordhelps them and rescues them;
