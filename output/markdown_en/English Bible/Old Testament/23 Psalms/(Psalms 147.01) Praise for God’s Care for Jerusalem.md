# Praise for God’s Care for Jerusalem

1. Praise theLord!
2. TheLordbuilds up Jerusalem;
3. He heals the brokenhearted,
4. He determines the number of the stars;
5. Great is our Lord, and abundant in power;
6. TheLordlifts up the downtrodden;
7. Sing to theLordwith thanksgiving;
8. He covers the heavens with clouds,
9. He gives to the animals their food,
10. His delight is not in the strength of the horse,
11. but theLordtakes pleasure in those who fear him,
12. Praise theLord, O Jerusalem!
13. For he strengthens the bars of your gates;
14. He grants peacewithin your borders;
15. He sends out his command to the earth;
16. He gives snow like wool;
17. He hurls down hail like crumbs—
18. He sends out his word, and melts them;
19. He declares his word to Jacob,
20. He has not dealt thus with any other nation;
