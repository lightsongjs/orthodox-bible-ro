# Prayer for Guidance and for Deliverance

1. To you, OLord, I lift up my soul.
2. O my God, in you I trust;
3. Do not let those who wait for you be put to shame;
4. Make me to know your ways, OLord;
5. Lead me in your truth, and teach me,
6. Be mindful of your mercy, OLord, and of your steadfast love,
7. Do not remember the sins of my youth or my transgressions;
8. Good and upright is theLord;
9. He leads the humble in what is right,
10. All the paths of theLordare steadfast love and faithfulness,
11. For your name’s sake, OLord,
12. Who are they that fear theLord?
13. They will abide in prosperity,
14. The friendship of theLordis for those who fear him,
15. My eyes are ever toward theLord,
16. Turn to me and be gracious to me,
17. Relieve the troubles of my heart,
18. Consider my affliction and my trouble,
19. Consider how many are my foes,
20. O guard my life, and deliver me;
21. May integrity and uprightness preserve me,
22. Redeem Israel, O God,
