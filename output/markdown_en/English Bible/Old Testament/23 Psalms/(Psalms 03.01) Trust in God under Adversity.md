# Trust in God under Adversity

1. OLord, how many are my foes!
2. many are saying to me,
3. But you, OLord, are a shield around me,
4. I cry aloud to theLord,
5. I lie down and sleep;
6. I am not afraid of ten thousands of people
7. Rise up, OLord!
8. Deliverance belongs to theLord;
