# Praise to God Who Comes in Judgment

1. O sing to theLorda new song;
2. Sing to theLord, bless his name;
3. Declare his glory among the nations,
4. For great is theLord, and greatly to be praised;
5. For all the gods of the peoples are idols,
6. Honor and majesty are before him;
7. Ascribe to theLord, O families of the peoples,
8. Ascribe to theLordthe glory due his name;
9. Worship theLordin holy splendor;
10. Say among the nations, “TheLordis king!
11. Let the heavens be glad, and let the earth rejoice;
12. let the field exult, and everything in it.
