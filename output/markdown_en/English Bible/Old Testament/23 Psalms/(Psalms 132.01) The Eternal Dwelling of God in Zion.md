# The Eternal Dwelling of God in Zion

1. OLord, remember in David’s favor
2. how he swore to theLord
3. “I will not enter my house
4. I will not give sleep to my eyes
5. until I find a place for theLord,
6. We heard of it in Ephrathah;
7. “Let us go to his dwelling place;
8. Rise up, OLord, and go to your resting place,
9. Let your priests be clothed with righteousness,
10. For your servant David’s sake
11. TheLordswore to David a sure oath
12. If your sons keep my covenant
13. For theLordhas chosen Zion;
14. “This is my resting place forever;
15. I will abundantly bless its provisions;
16. Its priests I will clothe with salvation,
17. There I will cause a horn to sprout up for David;
18. His enemies I will clothe with disgrace,
