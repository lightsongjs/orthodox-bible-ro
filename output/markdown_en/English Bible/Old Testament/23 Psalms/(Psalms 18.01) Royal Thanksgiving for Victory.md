# Royal Thanksgiving for Victory

1. I love you, OLord, my strength.
2. TheLordis my rock, my fortress, and my deliverer,
3. I call upon theLord, who is worthy to be praised,
4. The cords of death encompassed me;
5. the cords of Sheol entangled me;
6. In my distress I called upon theLord;
7. Then the earth reeled and rocked;
8. Smoke went up from his nostrils,
9. He bowed the heavens, and came down;
10. He rode on a cherub, and flew;
11. He made darkness his covering around him,
12. Out of the brightness before him
13. TheLordalso thundered in the heavens,
14. And he sent out his arrows, and scattered them;
15. Then the channels of the sea were seen,
16. He reached down from on high, he took me;
17. He delivered me from my strong enemy,
18. They confronted me in the day of my calamity;
19. He brought me out into a broad place;
20. TheLordrewarded me according to my righteousness;
21. For I have kept the ways of theLord,
22. For all his ordinances were before me,
23. I was blameless before him,
24. Therefore theLordhas recompensed me according to my righteousness,
25. With the loyal you show yourself loyal;
26. with the pure you show yourself pure;
27. For you deliver a humble people,
28. It is you who light my lamp;
29. By you I can crush a troop,
30. This God—his way is perfect;
31. For who is God except theLord?
32. the God who girded me with strength,
33. He made my feet like the feet of a deer,
34. He trains my hands for war,
35. You have given me the shield of your salvation,
36. You gave me a wide place for my steps under me,
37. I pursued my enemies and overtook them;
38. I struck them down, so that they were not able to rise;
39. For you girded me with strength for the battle;
40. You made my enemies turn their backs to me,
41. They cried for help, but there was no one to save them;
42. I beat them fine, like dust before the wind;
43. You delivered me from strife with the peoples;
44. As soon as they heard of me they obeyed me;
45. Foreigners lost heart,
46. TheLordlives! Blessed be my rock,
47. the God who gave me vengeance
48. who delivered me from my enemies;
49. For this I will extol you, OLord, among the nations,
50. Great triumphs he gives to his king,
