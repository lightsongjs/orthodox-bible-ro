# A Confession of Israel’s Sins

1. Praise theLord!
2. Who can utter the mighty doings of theLord,
3. Happy are those who observe justice,
4. Remember me, OLord, when you show favor to your people;
5. that I may see the prosperity of your chosen ones,
6. Both we and our ancestors have sinned;
7. Our ancestors, when they were in Egypt,
8. Yet he saved them for his name’s sake,
9. He rebuked the Red Sea,and it became dry;
10. So he saved them from the hand of the foe,
11. The waters covered their adversaries;
12. Then they believed his words;
13. But they soon forgot his works;
14. But they had a wanton craving in the wilderness,
15. he gave them what they asked,
16. They were jealous of Moses in the camp,
17. The earth opened and swallowed up Dathan,
18. Fire also broke out in their company;
19. They made a calf at Horeb
20. They exchanged the glory of God
21. They forgot God, their Savior,
22. wondrous works in the land of Ham,
23. Therefore he said he would destroy them—
24. Then they despised the pleasant land,
25. They grumbled in their tents,
26. Therefore he raised his hand and swore to them
27. and would dispersetheir descendants among the nations,
28. Then they attached themselves to the Baal of Peor,
29. they provoked theLordto anger with their deeds,
30. Then Phinehas stood up and interceded,
31. And that has been reckoned to him as righteousness
32. They angered theLordat the waters of Meribah,
33. for they made his spirit bitter,
34. They did not destroy the peoples,
35. but they mingled with the nations
36. They served their idols,
37. They sacrificed their sons
38. they poured out innocent blood,
39. Thus they became unclean by their acts,
40. Then the anger of theLordwas kindled against his people,
41. he gave them into the hand of the nations,
42. Their enemies oppressed them,
43. Many times he delivered them,
44. Nevertheless he regarded their distress
45. For their sake he remembered his covenant,
46. He caused them to be pitied
47. Save us, OLordour God,
48. Blessed be theLord, the God of Israel,
