# Plea for Help against Persecutors

1. OLordmy God, in you I take refuge;
2. or like a lion they will tear me apart;
3. OLordmy God, if I have done this,
4. if I have repaid my ally with harm
5. then let the enemy pursue and overtake me,
6. Rise up, OLord, in your anger;
7. Let the assembly of the peoples be gathered around you,
8. TheLordjudges the peoples;
9. O let the evil of the wicked come to an end,
10. God is my shield,
11. God is a righteous judge,
12. If one does not repent, Godwill whet his sword;
13. he has prepared his deadly weapons,
14. See how they conceive evil,
15. They make a pit, digging it out,
16. Their mischief returns upon their own heads,
17. I will give to theLordthe thanks due to his righteousness,
