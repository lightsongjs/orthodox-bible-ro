# Thanksgiving and Praise

1. I give you thanks, OLord, with my whole heart;
2. I bow down toward your holy temple
3. On the day I called, you answered me,
4. All the kings of the earth shall praise you, OLord,
5. They shall sing of the ways of theLord,
6. For though theLordis high, he regards the lowly;
7. Though I walk in the midst of trouble,
8. TheLordwill fulfill his purpose for me;
