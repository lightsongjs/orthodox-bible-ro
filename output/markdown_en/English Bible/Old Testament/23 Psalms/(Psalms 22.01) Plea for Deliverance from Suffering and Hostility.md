# Plea for Deliverance from Suffering and Hostility

1. My God, my God, why have you forsaken me?
2. O my God, I cry by day, but you do not answer;
3. Yet you are holy,
4. In you our ancestors trusted;
5. To you they cried, and were saved;
6. But I am a worm, and not human;
7. All who see me mock at me;
8. “Commit your cause to theLord; let him deliver—
9. Yet it was you who took me from the womb;
10. On you I was cast from my birth,
11. Do not be far from me,
12. Many bulls encircle me,
13. they open wide their mouths at me,
14. I am poured out like water,
15. my mouthis dried up like a potsherd,
16. For dogs are all around me;
17. I can count all my bones.
18. they divide my clothes among themselves,
19. But you, OLord, do not be far away!
20. Deliver my soul from the sword,
21. Save me from the mouth of the lion!
22. I will tell of your name to my brothers and sisters;
23. You who fear theLord, praise him!
24. For he did not despise or abhor
25. From you comes my praise in the great congregation;
26. The poorshall eat and be satisfied;
27. All the ends of the earth shall remember
28. For dominion belongs to theLord,
29. To him,indeed, shall all who sleep inthe earth bow down;
30. Posterity will serve him;
31. andproclaim his deliverance to a people yet unborn,
