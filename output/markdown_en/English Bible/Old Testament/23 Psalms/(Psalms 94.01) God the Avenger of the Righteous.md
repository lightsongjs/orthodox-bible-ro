# God the Avenger of the Righteous

1. OLord, you God of vengeance,
2. Rise up, O judge of the earth;
3. OLord, how long shall the wicked,
4. They pour out their arrogant words;
5. They crush your people, OLord,
6. They kill the widow and the stranger,
7. and they say, “TheLorddoes not see;
8. Understand, O dullest of the people;
9. He who planted the ear, does he not hear?
10. He who disciplines the nations,
11. TheLordknows our thoughts,
12. Happy are those whom you discipline, OLord,
13. giving them respite from days of trouble,
14. For theLordwill not forsake his people;
15. for justice will return to the righteous,
16. Who rises up for me against the wicked?
17. If theLordhad not been my help,
18. When I thought, “My foot is slipping,”
19. When the cares of my heart are many,
20. Can wicked rulers be allied with you,
21. They band together against the life of the righteous,
22. But theLordhas become my stronghold,
23. He will repay them for their iniquity
