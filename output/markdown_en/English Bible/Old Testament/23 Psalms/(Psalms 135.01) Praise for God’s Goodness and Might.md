# Praise for God’s Goodness and Might

1. Praise theLord!
2. you that stand in the house of theLord,
3. Praise theLord, for theLordis good;
4. For theLordhas chosen Jacob for himself,
5. For I know that theLordis great;
6. Whatever theLordpleases he does,
7. He it is who makes the clouds rise at the end of the earth;
8. He it was who struck down the firstborn of Egypt,
9. he sent signs and wonders
10. He struck down many nations
11. Sihon, king of the Amorites,
12. and gave their land as a heritage,
13. Your name, OLord, endures forever,
14. For theLordwill vindicate his people,
15. The idols of the nations are silver and gold,
16. They have mouths, but they do not speak;
17. they have ears, but they do not hear,
18. Those who make them
19. O house of Israel, bless theLord!
20. O house of Levi, bless theLord!
21. Blessed be theLordfrom Zion,
