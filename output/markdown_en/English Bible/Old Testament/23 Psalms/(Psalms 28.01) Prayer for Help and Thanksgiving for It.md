# Prayer for Help and Thanksgiving for It

1. To you, OLord, I call;
2. Hear the voice of my supplication,
3. Do not drag me away with the wicked,
4. Repay them according to their work,
5. Because they do not regard the works of theLord,
6. Blessed be theLord,
7. TheLordis my strength and my shield;
8. TheLordis the strength of his people;
9. O save your people, and bless your heritage;
