# Prayer for Deliverance from Slanderers

1. In my distress I cry to theLord,
2. “Deliver me, OLord,
3. What shall be given to you?
4. A warrior’s sharp arrows,
5. Woe is me, that I am an alien in Meshech,
6. Too long have I had my dwelling
7. I am for peace;
