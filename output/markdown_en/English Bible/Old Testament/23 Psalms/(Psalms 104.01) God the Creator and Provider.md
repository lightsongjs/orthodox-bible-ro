# God the Creator and Provider

1. Bless theLord, O my soul.
2. wrapped in light as with a garment.
3. you set the beams of yourchambers on the waters,
4. you make the winds yourmessengers,
5. You set the earth on its foundations,
6. You cover it with the deep as with a garment;
7. At your rebuke they flee;
8. They rose up to the mountains, ran down to the valleys
9. You set a boundary that they may not pass,
10. You make springs gush forth in the valleys;
11. giving drink to every wild animal;
12. By the streamsthe birds of the air have their habitation;
13. From your lofty abode you water the mountains;
14. You cause the grass to grow for the cattle,
15. and wine to gladden the human heart,
16. The trees of theLordare watered abundantly,
17. In them the birds build their nests;
18. The high mountains are for the wild goats;
19. You have made the moon to mark the seasons;
20. You make darkness, and it is night,
21. The young lions roar for their prey,
22. When the sun rises, they withdraw
23. People go out to their work
24. OLord, how manifold are your works!
25. Yonder is the sea, great and wide,
26. There go the ships,
27. These all look to you
28. when you give to them, they gather it up;
29. When you hide your face, they are dismayed;
30. When you send forth your spirit,they are created;
31. May the glory of theLordendure forever;
32. who looks on the earth and it trembles,
33. I will sing to theLordas long as I live;
34. May my meditation be pleasing to him,
35. Let sinners be consumed from the earth,
