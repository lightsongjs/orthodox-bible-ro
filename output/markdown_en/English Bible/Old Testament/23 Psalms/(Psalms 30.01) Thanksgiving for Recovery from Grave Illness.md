# Thanksgiving for Recovery from Grave Illness

1. I will extol you, OLord, for you have drawn me up,
2. OLordmy God, I cried to you for help,
3. OLord, you brought up my soul from Sheol,
4. Sing praises to theLord, O you his faithful ones,
5. For his anger is but for a moment;
6. As for me, I said in my prosperity,
7. By your favor, OLord,
8. To you, OLord, I cried,
9. “What profit is there in my death,
10. Hear, OLord, and be gracious to me!
11. You have turned my mourning into dancing;
12. so that my soulmay praise you and not be silent.
