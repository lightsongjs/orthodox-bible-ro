# Prayer and Praise for Deliverance from Enemies

1. In you, OLord, I seek refuge;
2. Incline your ear to me;
3. You are indeed my rock and my fortress;
4. take me out of the net that is hidden for me,
5. Into your hand I commit my spirit;
6. You hatethose who pay regard to worthless idols,
7. I will exult and rejoice in your steadfast love,
8. and have not delivered me into the hand of the enemy;
9. Be gracious to me, OLord, for I am in distress;
10. For my life is spent with sorrow,
11. I am the scorn of all my adversaries,
12. I have passed out of mind like one who is dead;
13. For I hear the whispering of many—
14. But I trust in you, OLord;
15. My times are in your hand;
16. Let your face shine upon your servant;
17. Do not let me be put to shame, OLord,
18. Let the lying lips be stilled
19. O how abundant is your goodness
20. In the shelter of your presence you hide them
21. Blessed be theLord,
22. I had said in my alarm,
23. Love theLord, all you his saints.
24. Be strong, and let your heart take courage,
