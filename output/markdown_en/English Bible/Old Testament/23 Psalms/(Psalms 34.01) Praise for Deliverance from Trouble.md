# Praise for Deliverance from Trouble

1. I will bless theLordat all times;
2. My soul makes its boast in theLord;
3. O magnify theLordwith me,
4. I sought theLord, and he answered me,
5. Look to him, and be radiant;
6. This poor soul cried, and was heard by theLord,
7. The angel of theLordencamps
8. O taste and see that theLordis good;
9. O fear theLord, you his holy ones,
10. The young lions suffer want and hunger,
11. Come, O children, listen to me;
12. Which of you desires life,
13. Keep your tongue from evil,
14. Depart from evil, and do good;
15. The eyes of theLordare on the righteous,
16. The face of theLordis against evildoers,
17. When the righteous cry for help, theLordhears,
18. TheLordis near to the brokenhearted,
19. Many are the afflictions of the righteous,
20. He keeps all their bones;
21. Evil brings death to the wicked,
22. TheLordredeems the life of his servants;
