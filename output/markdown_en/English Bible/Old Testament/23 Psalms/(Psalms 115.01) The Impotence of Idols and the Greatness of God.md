# The Impotence of Idols and the Greatness of God

1. Not to us, OLord, not to us, but to your name give glory,
2. Why should the nations say,
3. Our God is in the heavens;
4. Their idols are silver and gold,
5. They have mouths, but do not speak;
6. They have ears, but do not hear;
7. They have hands, but do not feel;
8. Those who make them are like them;
9. O Israel, trust in theLord!
10. O house of Aaron, trust in theLord!
11. You who fear theLord, trust in theLord!
12. TheLordhas been mindful of us; he will bless us;
13. he will bless those who fear theLord,
14. May theLordgive you increase,
15. May you be blessed by theLord,
16. The heavens are theLord’s heavens,
17. The dead do not praise theLord,
18. But we will bless theLord
