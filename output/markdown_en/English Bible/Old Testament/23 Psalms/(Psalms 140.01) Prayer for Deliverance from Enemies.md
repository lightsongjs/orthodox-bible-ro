# Prayer for Deliverance from Enemies

1. Deliver me, OLord, from evildoers;
2. who plan evil things in their minds
3. They make their tongue sharp as a snake’s,
4. Guard me, OLord, from the hands of the wicked;
5. The arrogant have hidden a trap for me,
6. I say to theLord, “You are my God;
7. OLord, my Lord, my strong deliverer,
8. Do not grant, OLord, the desires of the wicked;
9. Those who surround me lift up their heads;
10. Let burning coals fall on them!
11. Do not let the slanderer be established in the land;
12. I know that theLordmaintains the cause of the needy,
13. Surely the righteous shall give thanks to your name;
