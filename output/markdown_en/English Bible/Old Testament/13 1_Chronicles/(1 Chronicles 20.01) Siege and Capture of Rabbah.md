# Siege and Capture of Rabbah

20. In the spring of the year, the time when kings go out to battle, Joab led out the army, ravaged the country of the Ammonites, and came and besieged Rabbah. But David remained at Jerusalem. Joab attacked Rabbah, and overthrew it.
2. David took the crown of Milcomfrom his head; he found that it weighed a talent of gold, and in it was a precious stone; and it was placed on David’s head. He also brought out the booty of the city, a very great amount.
3. He brought out the people who were in it, and set them to workwith saws and iron picks and axes.Thus David did to all the cities of the Ammonites. Then David and all the people returned to Jerusalem.
