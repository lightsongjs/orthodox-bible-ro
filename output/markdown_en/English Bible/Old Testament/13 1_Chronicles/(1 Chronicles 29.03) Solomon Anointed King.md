# Solomon Anointed King

23. Then Solomon sat on the throne of theLord, succeeding his father David as king; he prospered, and all Israel obeyed him.
24. All the leaders and the mighty warriors, and also all the sons of King David, pledged their allegiance to King Solomon.
25. TheLordhighly exalted Solomon in the sight of all Israel, and bestowed upon him such royal majesty as had not been on any king before him in Israel.
