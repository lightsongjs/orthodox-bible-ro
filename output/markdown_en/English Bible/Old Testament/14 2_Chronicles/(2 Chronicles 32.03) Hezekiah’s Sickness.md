# Hezekiah’s Sickness

24. In those days Hezekiah became sick and was at the point of death. He prayed to theLord, and he answered him and gave him a sign.
25. But Hezekiah did not respond according to the benefit done to him, for his heart was proud. Therefore wrath came upon him and upon Judah and Jerusalem.
26. Then Hezekiah humbled himself for the pride of his heart, both he and the inhabitants of Jerusalem, so that the wrath of theLorddid not come upon them in the days of Hezekiah.
