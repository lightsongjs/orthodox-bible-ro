# Reign and Captivity of Jehoiakim

5. Jehoiakim was twenty-five years old when he began to reign; he reigned eleven years in Jerusalem. He did what was evil in the sight of theLordhis God.
6. Against him King Nebuchadnezzar of Babylon came up, and bound him with fetters to take him to Babylon.
7. Nebuchadnezzar also carried some of the vessels of the house of theLordto Babylon and put them in his palace in Babylon.
8. Now the rest of the acts of Jehoiakim, and the abominations that he did, and what was found against him, are written in the Book of the Kings of Israel and Judah; and his son Jehoiachin succeeded him.
