# Many Peoples Drawn to Jerusalem

20. Thus says theLordof hosts: Peoples shall yet come, the inhabitants of many cities;
21. the inhabitants of one city shall go to another, saying, “Come, let us go to entreat the favor of theLord, and to seek theLordof hosts; I myself am going.”
22. Many peoples and strong nations shall come to seek theLordof hosts in Jerusalem, and to entreat the favor of theLord.
23. Thus says theLordof hosts: In those days ten men from nations of every language shall take hold of a Jew, grasping his garment and saying, “Let us go with you, for we have heard that God is with you.”
