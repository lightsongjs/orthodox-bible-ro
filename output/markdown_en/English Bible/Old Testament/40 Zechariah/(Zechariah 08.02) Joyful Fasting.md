# Joyful Fasting

18. The word of theLordof hosts came to me, saying:
19. Thus says theLordof hosts: The fast of the fourth month, and the fast of the fifth, and the fast of the seventh, and the fast of the tenth, shall be seasons of joy and gladness, and cheerful festivals for the house of Judah: therefore love truth and peace.
