# Judith Is Brought before Holofernes

20. Then the guards of Holofernes and all his servants came out and led her into the tent.
21. Holofernes was resting on his bed under a canopy that was woven with purple and gold, emeralds and other precious stones.
22. When they told him of her, he came to the front of the tent, with silver lamps carried before him.
23. When Judith came into the presence of Holofernesand his servants, they all marveled at the beauty of her face. She prostrated herself and did obeisance to him, but his slaves raised her up.
