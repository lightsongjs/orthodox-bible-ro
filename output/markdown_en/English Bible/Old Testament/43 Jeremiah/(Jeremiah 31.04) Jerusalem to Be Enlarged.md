# Jerusalem to Be Enlarged

38. The days are surely coming, says theLord, when the city shall be rebuilt for theLordfrom the tower of Hananel to the Corner Gate.
39. And the measuring line shall go out farther, straight to the hill Gareb, and shall then turn to Goah.
40. The whole valley of the dead bodies and the ashes, and all the fields as far as the Wadi Kidron, to the corner of the Horse Gate toward the east, shall be sacred to theLord. It shall never again be uprooted or overthrown.
