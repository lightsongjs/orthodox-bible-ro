# Symbol of the Wine-Jars

12. You shall speak to them this word: Thus says theLord, the God of Israel: Every wine-jar should be filled with wine. And they will say to you, “Do you think we do not know that every wine-jar should be filled with wine?”
13. Then you shall say to them: Thus says theLord: I am about to fill all the inhabitants of this land—the kings who sit on David’s throne, the priests, the prophets, and all the inhabitants of Jerusalem—with drunkenness.
14. And I will dash them one against another, parents and children together, says theLord. I will not pity or spare or have compassion when I destroy them.
