# The Great Drought

14. The word of theLordthat came to Jeremiah concerning the drought:
2. Judah mourns
3. Her nobles send their servants for water;
4. because the ground is cracked.
5. Even the doe in the field forsakes her newborn fawn
6. The wild asses stand on the bare heights,
7. Although our iniquities testify against us,
8. O hope of Israel,
9. Why should you be like someone confused,
10. Thus says theLordconcerning this people:
11. TheLordsaid to me: Do not pray for the welfare of this people.
12. Although they fast, I do not hear their cry, and although they offer burnt offering and grain offering, I do not accept them; but by the sword, by famine, and by pestilence I consume them.
