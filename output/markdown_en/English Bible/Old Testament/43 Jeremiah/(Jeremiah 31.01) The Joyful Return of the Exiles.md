# The Joyful Return of the Exiles

31. At that time, says theLord, I will be the God of all the families of Israel, and they shall be my people.
2. Thus says theLord:
3. theLordappeared to himfrom far away.
4. Again I will build you, and you shall be built,
5. Again you shall plant vineyards
6. For there shall be a day when sentinels will call
7. For thus says theLord:
8. See, I am going to bring them from the land of the north,
9. With weeping they shall come,
10. Hear the word of theLord, O nations,
11. For theLordhas ransomed Jacob,
12. They shall come and sing aloud on the height of Zion,
13. Then shall the young women rejoice in the dance,
14. I will give the priests their fill of fatness,
15. Thus says theLord:
16. Thus says theLord:
17. there is hope for your future,
18. Indeed I heard Ephraim pleading:
19. For after I had turned away I repented;
20. Is Ephraim my dear son?
21. Set up road markers for yourself,
22. How long will you waver,
23. Thus says theLordof hosts, the God of Israel: Once more they shall use these words in the land of Judah and in its towns when I restore their fortunes:
24. And Judah and all its towns shall live there together, and the farmers and those who wanderwith their flocks.
25. I will satisfy the weary,
26. Thereupon I awoke and looked, and my sleep was pleasant to me.
