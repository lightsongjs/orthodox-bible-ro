# Jeremiah’s Life Threatened

18. It was theLordwho made it known to me, and I knew;
19. But I was like a gentle lamb
20. But you, OLordof hosts, who judge righteously,
21. Therefore thus says theLordconcerning the people of Anathoth, who seek your life, and say, “You shall not prophesy in the name of theLord, or you will die by our hand”—
22. therefore thus says theLordof hosts: I am going to punish them; the young men shall die by the sword; their sons and their daughters shall die by famine;
23. and not even a remnant shall be left of them. For I will bring disaster upon the people of Anathoth, the year of their punishment.
