# God Replies to Jeremiah

5. If you have raced with foot-runners and they have wearied you,
6. For even your kinsfolk and your own family,
7. I have forsaken my house,
8. My heritage has become to me
9. Is the hyena greedyfor my heritage at my command?
10. Many shepherds have destroyed my vineyard,
11. They have made it a desolation;
12. Upon all the bare heightsin the desert
13. They have sown wheat and have reaped thorns,
14. Thus says theLordconcerning all my evil neighbors who touch the heritage that I have given my people Israel to inherit: I am about to pluck them up from their land, and I will pluck up the house of Judah from among them.
15. And after I have plucked them up, I will again have compassion on them, and I will bring them again to their heritage and to their land, every one of them.
16. And then, if they will diligently learn the ways of my people, to swear by my name, “As theLordlives,” as they taught my people to swear by Baal, then they shall be built up in the midst of my people.
17. But if any nation will not listen, then I will completely uproot it and destroy it, says theLord.
