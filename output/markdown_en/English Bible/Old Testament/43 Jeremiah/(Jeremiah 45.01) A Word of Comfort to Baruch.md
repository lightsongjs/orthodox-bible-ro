# A Word of Comfort to Baruch

45. The word that the prophet Jeremiah spoke to Baruch son of Neriah, when he wrote these words in a scroll at the dictation of Jeremiah, in the fourth year of King Jehoiakim son of Josiah of Judah:
2. Thus says theLord, the God of Israel, to you, O Baruch:
3. You said, “Woe is me! TheLordhas added sorrow to my pain; I am weary with my groaning, and I find no rest.”
4. Thus you shall say to him, “Thus says theLord: I am going to break down what I have built, and pluck up what I have planted—that is, the whole land.
5. And you, do you seek great things for yourself? Do not seek them; for I am going to bring disaster upon all flesh, says theLord; but I will give you your life as a prize of war in every place to which you may go.”
