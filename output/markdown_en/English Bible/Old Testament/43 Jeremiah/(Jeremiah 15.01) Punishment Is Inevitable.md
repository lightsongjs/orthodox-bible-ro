# Punishment Is Inevitable

15. Then theLordsaid to me: Though Moses and Samuel stood before me, yet my heart would not turn toward this people. Send them out of my sight, and let them go!
2. And when they say to you, “Where shall we go?” you shall say to them: Thus says theLord:
3. And I will appoint over them four kinds of destroyers, says theLord: the sword to kill, the dogs to drag away, and the birds of the air and the wild animals of the earth to devour and destroy.
4. I will make them a horror to all the kingdoms of the earth because of what King Manasseh son of Hezekiah of Judah did in Jerusalem.
5. Who will have pity on you, O Jerusalem,
6. You have rejected me, says theLord,
7. I have winnowed them with a winnowing fork
8. Their widows became more numerous
9. She who bore seven has languished;
