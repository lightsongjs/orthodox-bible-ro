# The Consuming Wrath of God

2. A jealous and avenging God is theLord,
3. TheLordis slow to anger but great in power,
4. He rebukes the sea and makes it dry,
5. The mountains quake before him,
6. Who can stand before his indignation?
7. TheLordis good,
8. even in a rushing flood.
9. Why do you plot against theLord?
10. Like thorns they are entangled,
11. From you one has gone out
