# The Destruction of the Wicked City

2. A shattererhas come up against you.
2. (For theLordis restoring the majesty of Jacob,
3. The shields of his warriors are red;
4. The chariots race madly through the streets,
5. He calls his officers;
6. The river gates are opened,
7. It is decreedthat the citybe exiled,
8. Nineveh is like a pool
9. “Plunder the silver,
10. Devastation, desolation, and destruction!
11. What became of the lions’ den,
12. The lion has torn enough for his whelps
13. See, I am against you, says theLordof hosts, and I will burn yourchariots in smoke, and the sword shall devour your young lions; I will cut off your prey from the earth, and the voice of your messengers shall be heard no more.
