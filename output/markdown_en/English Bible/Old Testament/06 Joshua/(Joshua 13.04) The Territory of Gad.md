# The Territory of Gad

24. Moses gave an inheritance also to the tribe of the Gadites, according to their families.
25. Their territory was Jazer, and all the towns of Gilead, and half the land of the Ammonites, to Aroer, which is east of Rabbah,
26. and from Heshbon to Ramath-mizpeh and Betonim, and from Mahanaim to the territory of Debir,
27. and in the valley Beth-haram, Beth-nimrah, Succoth, and Zaphon, the rest of the kingdom of King Sihon of Heshbon, the Jordan and its banks, as far as the lower end of the Sea of Chinnereth, eastward beyond the Jordan.
28. This is the inheritance of the Gadites according to their clans, with their towns and villages.
