# David’s Officers

15. So David reigned over all Israel; and David administered justice and equity to all his people.
16. Joab son of Zeruiah was over the army; Jehoshaphat son of Ahilud was recorder;
17. Zadok son of Ahitub and Ahimelech son of Abiathar were priests; Seraiah was secretary;
18. Benaiah son of Jehoiada was overthe Cherethites and the Pelethites; and David’s sons were priests.
