# The Plot to Kill Jesus

14. It was two days before the Passover and the festival of Unleavened Bread. The chief priests and the scribes were looking for a way to arrest Jesusby stealth and kill him;
2. for they said, “Not during the festival, or there may be a riot among the people.”
