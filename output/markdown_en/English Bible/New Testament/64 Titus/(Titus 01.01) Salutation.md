# Salutation

1. Paul, a servantof God and an apostle of Jesus Christ, for the sake of the faith of God’s elect and the knowledge of the truth that is in accordance with godliness,
2. in the hope of eternal life that God, who never lies, promised before the ages began—
3. in due time he revealed his word through the proclamation with which I have been entrusted by the command of God our Savior,
4. To Titus, my loyal child in the faith we share:
