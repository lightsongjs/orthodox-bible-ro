# Final Doxology

25. Now to Godwho is able to strengthen you according to my gospel and the proclamation of Jesus Christ, according to the revelation of the mystery that was kept secret for long ages
26. but is now disclosed, and through the prophetic writings is made known to all the Gentiles, according to the command of the eternal God, to bring about the obedience of faith—
27. to the only wise God, through Jesus Christ, to whombe the glory forever! Amen.
