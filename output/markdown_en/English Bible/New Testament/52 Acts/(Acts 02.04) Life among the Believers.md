# Life among the Believers

43. Awe came upon everyone, because many wonders and signs were being done by the apostles.
44. All who believed were together and had all things in common;
45. they would sell their possessions and goods and distribute the proceedsto all, as any had need.
46. Day by day, as they spent much time together in the temple, they broke bread at homeand ate their food with glad and generoushearts,
47. praising God and having the goodwill of all the people. And day by day the Lord added to their number those who were being saved.
