# Blessings and Woes

20. Then he looked up at his disciples and said:
21. “Blessed are you who are hungry now,
22. “Blessed are you when people hate you, and when they exclude you, revile you, and defame youon account of the Son of Man.
23. Rejoice in that day and leap for joy, for surely your reward is great in heaven; for that is what their ancestors did to the prophets.
24. “But woe to you who are rich,
25. “Woe to you who are full now,
26. “Woe to you when all speak well of you, for that is what their ancestors did to the false prophets.
