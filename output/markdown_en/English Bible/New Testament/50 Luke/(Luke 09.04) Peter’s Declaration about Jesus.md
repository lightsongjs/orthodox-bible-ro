# Peter’s Declaration about Jesus

18. Once when Jesuswas praying alone, with only the disciples near him, he asked them, “Who do the crowds say that I am?”
19. They answered, “John the Baptist; but others, Elijah; and still others, that one of the ancient prophets has arisen.”
20. He said to them, “But who do you say that I am?” Peter answered, “The Messiahof God.”
