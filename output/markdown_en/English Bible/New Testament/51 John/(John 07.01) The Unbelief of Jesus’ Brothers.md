# The Unbelief of Jesus’ Brothers

7. After this Jesus went about in Galilee. He did not wishto go about in Judea because the Jews were looking for an opportunity to kill him.
2. Now the Jewish festival of Boothswas near.
3. So his brothers said to him, “Leave here and go to Judea so that your disciples also may see the works you are doing;
4. for no one who wantsto be widely known acts in secret. If you do these things, show yourself to the world.”
5. (For not even his brothers believed in him.)
6. Jesus said to them, “My time has not yet come, but your time is always here.
7. The world cannot hate you, but it hates me because I testify against it that its works are evil.
8. Go to the festival yourselves. I am notgoing to this festival, for my time has not yet fully come.”
9. After saying this, he remained in Galilee.
