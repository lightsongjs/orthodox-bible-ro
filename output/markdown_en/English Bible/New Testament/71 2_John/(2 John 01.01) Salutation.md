# Salutation

1. The elder to the elect lady and her children, whom I love in the truth, and not only I but also all who know the truth,
2. because of the truth that abides in us and will be with us forever:
3. Grace, mercy, and peace will be with us from God the Father and fromJesus Christ, the Father’s Son, in truth and love.
